The Case for Vitamin D3 and K2 Together

Vitamin D gets most of the attention and vitamin K gets very little. Why the two are often paired, what they're authorised to claim and how much vitamin D UK adults need.

Vitamin D gets plenty of attention, especially once the clocks go back. Vitamin K gets almost none, which is odd for a vitamin that shares one of D's main jobs.

Why they're paired

Vitamin D helps your body absorb calcium. Vitamin K2 is involved in activating proteins that help put calcium to use in bone. That's why you'll often see the two sold together.

They share an authorised claim, and each has one of its own:[1]

  • ✓ Vitamin D contributes to the maintenance of normal bones.
  • ✓ Vitamin K contributes to the maintenance of normal bones.
  • ✓ Vitamin D contributes to the normal function of the immune system.
  • ✓ Vitamin K contributes to normal blood clotting.

Which K2?

Vitamin K2 comes in several forms. MK-7 (menaquinone-7) is made by fermentation and stays in the bloodstream longer than other forms. Scōp uses vitamin K2 as MK-7, at 100 µg per serving.

The UK vitamin D situation

From October to March, the sun in the UK isn't strong enough for your skin to make much vitamin D. The NHS says everyone should consider taking a daily vitamin D supplement during autumn and winter.[2]

Summer doesn't always fix it either. If most of your daylight hours are spent at a desk, you may not get much sun even in July.

How much vitamin D?

The NHS says adults need 10 µg (400 IU) of vitamin D a day. It also says not to take more than 100 µg (4,000 IU) a day, as it could be harmful.[2]

Scōp contains 100 µg (4,000 IU) of vitamin D3 per serving, which is the NHS maximum. If you take Scōp, you don't need another vitamin D supplement on top.

Bones are a long game

Bone is maintained over decades, which is why steady daily intake matters more than an occasional burst. Scōp puts D3 and K2 in the same scoop, so you never have to remember two separate products.

Common questions

Why take vitamin D3 and K2 together?

Vitamin D helps you absorb calcium, and vitamin K2 is involved in activating proteins that put calcium to use in bone. Vitamin D contributes to the maintenance of normal bones. Vitamin K contributes to the maintenance of normal bones.

How much vitamin D do I need per day?

The NHS says adults need 10 µg (400 IU) a day and should not take more than 100 µg (4,000 IU) a day. Scōp contains 100 µg per serving.

Should I take vitamin D in the UK?

The NHS says everyone should consider a daily vitamin D supplement during autumn and winter. If you spend most of the day indoors, it's worth thinking about the rest of the year too.

What is vitamin K2 MK-7?

It's a form of vitamin K2 made by fermentation that stays in the bloodstream longer than other forms. Scōp contains 100 µg per serving.

Can you take too much vitamin D?

Yes. The NHS says not to take more than 100 µg (4,000 IU) a day, as it could be harmful. Scōp provides exactly that amount, so don't combine it with other vitamin D supplements.
