## What counts as an all in one supplement?

An all in one supplement is a single daily product, usually a powder mixed into water, that replaces a shelf of separate tubs. In the UK they tend to fall into two camps:

- **Greens style powders.** Long ingredient lists built around wellness: greens, fruit and vegetable extracts, probiotics, adaptogens and a spread of vitamins and minerals.
- **Targeted daily formulas.** Fewer ingredients, chosen for a clear job, with each one dosed to do it.

Neither is automatically better. The right one depends on what you want it to do. These six checks make it much easier to compare like with like.

## 1. Is every dose printed?

Look for the exact amount of every ingredient on the label. Some products group ingredients into a "proprietary blend" and only give the total weight of the blend. That makes it impossible to know whether a headline ingredient is there in a meaningful amount or a token sprinkle.

**What good looks like:** every ingredient listed with its own dose, on the pack and on the website.

## 2. Are the key doses meaningful?

More is not always better, but too little is just expensive decoration. A useful benchmark is NHS guidance for adults:

| Nutrient | NHS guidance for adults, from supplements |
|---|---|
| Vitamin D | Up to 100 µg (4,000 IU) a day<sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-1">[1]</a></sup> |
| Magnesium | Up to 400 mg a day<sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-2">[2]</a></sup> |
| Vitamin B6 | Up to 10 mg a day, unless a doctor advises otherwise<sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-3">[3]</a></sup> |

Check where a product sits against those figures. Some popular blends provide a fraction of them, which matters most for nutrients like vitamin D during the UK's darker months.

## 3. Does it cover performance as well as wellness?

Most all in one powders are built around wellness. If you train, the ingredient you're most likely to be buying separately is creatine.

Creatine **increases physical performance in successive bursts of short-term, high intensity exercise**, and the beneficial effect is obtained with a daily intake of 3 g<sup class="cite"><a href="#ref-4">[4]</a></sup>. That's a large amount compared with vitamins measured in micrograms, which is why it rarely appears in capsules or greens powders. A formula that includes a full daily dose of creatine can replace one more tub.

## 4. Are the ingredient forms named?

The form of a nutrient tells you a lot about how carefully a formula has been put together. Useful things to look for:

- **Magnesium** named by its form, such as magnesium bisglycinate, rather than just "magnesium"
- **Vitamin K2** as MK-7
- **Folate** as L-methylfolate and **vitamin B12** as methylcobalamin
- **Vitamin B6** as pyridoxal 5′-phosphate

If a label won't tell you the form, it's worth asking why.

## 5. What does it really cost per day?

Headline prices are hard to compare because pouch sizes and serving counts vary. Divide the price by the number of servings to get the true daily cost, and compare subscription with subscription.

When we compared subscription prices on the websites of several leading all in one blends in October 2026, they worked out at roughly £2.30 to £2.63 a day. Check whether a subscription can be paused or cancelled any time, and whether a "starter" price rises after the first order.

## 6. Will you actually take it?

The best formula in the world does nothing in the cupboard. Taste, how easily it mixes and whether it fits your morning all matter. For ingredients like creatine and vitamin D, what counts is what you take consistently over weeks, which is why one simple daily habit beats a complicated stack. ## Quick checklist

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Every dose printed | You know exactly what you're getting |
| Key doses measured against NHS guidance | Enough to be worth taking, within NHS guidance |
| Creatine included, if you train | One less product to buy and remember |
| Ingredient forms named | A sign the formula has been thought through |
| Cost per day on subscription | The only fair way to compare prices |
| Easy to take daily | Consistency is where the results come from |
