Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Scōp Supplement Ltd runs scopsupplement.uk and sells Scōp. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 17164565, and our registered office is 38 Ellesmere Road, Stockton Heath, Warrington, WA4 6DZ. We decide how the personal information on this page is used, which makes us the controller under UK data protection law. For anything to do with your data, email grae@scopsupplement.com.

What we collect and why

When you order

Your name, email, delivery address, what you bought, any gift message and any discount or referral code you used. Payment goes through Stripe, so we never see your full card details. We use this to take payment, send your order, run your subscription and keep the records the law asks of us. The lawful basis is our contract with you, and our legal obligations for the records.

If you subscribe, we give you a personal referral code. When someone uses it, we record that so the reward reaches the right account. We may also create a login for your Scōp account using your email address.

When you join the email list

Your email address, from the sign up form or the pop up. We use it to send the Weekly Scoop and offers, because you asked us to. If you start a checkout, tick the box to hear from us and don't finish, we may send you a reminder. If you buy from us, we may email you about Scōp and related products. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

When you use our form on Facebook or Instagram

Your email address, first name and whether you ticked the box to hear from us. We use it to send you what the form offered. We only add you to the email list if you ticked the box. Meta collects this for us, and its own privacy policy also applies.

When you leave a review

Your name, email address, star rating and review. We only publish your rating, review and first name with initial, and only because you agreed to it on the form. Your email address is never published. We use it only if we need to check something about your review.

When you ask about stocking Scōp

Your name, email, gym name, town, member numbers and message. We use them to reply and talk through working together. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the companies that run parts of Scōp for us:

  • Stripe, for payments, subscriptions and referral credits
  • Brevo, for our emails
  • Netlify, which hosts the site and handles our forms and account logins
  • Meta, for forms you fill in on Facebook or Instagram
  • TikTok, for analytics, but only if you accept cookies
  • Fontshare, which supplies the site's fonts and so sees your IP address when a page loads

Partners who give out a Scōp code don't receive your name or contact details from us. Some of these companies store data outside the UK. Each one's privacy policy explains how it protects that data.

Cookies

We use a few cookies and some browser storage that the site needs to work. These are always on:

  • scop_consent remembers your cookie choice for about six months
  • scop_offer_seen stops the pop up showing again for seven days
  • scop_promo holds a discount code from a link you followed until you close your browser
  • your basket and login status are kept in your browser's storage

The TikTok analytics cookies (_ttp, ttcsid and _tt_enable_cookie) are only set if you press Accept on the cookie banner. They tell us which ads and links bring people to the site. If you decline, they aren't set and the site works the same. To change your mind, clear this site's cookies and the banner will ask again.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as UK tax law requires. You stay on the email list until you unsubscribe. Everything else is kept only as long as we need it for the reason you gave it to us.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. You can also object to how we use it or ask us to send it to you or another company. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email grae@scopsupplement.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
